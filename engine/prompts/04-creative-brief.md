# Creative brief generator (weekly)
Inputs: this account's winners, the competitor pull, and the playbook.
For each winner, write 2-4 briefs that keep the winning angle and change one variable
(hook, format, proof, offer framing). For each "potential", write variations instead.
Rules:
- Name every asset with {{naming_convention}} so performance can be traced back to the angle.
- Meta suppresses creatives that are more than ~60% similar — push concept diversity, not volume.
- Real footage of the actual work or product. No AI imagery that misrepresents what is sold.
- Assets must be reachable at public URLs before the connector can upload them.
Output: briefs only, ready to shoot or design.
