# Creative fatigue + funnel break (weekly)
Last 21 days, by week, for {{ad_account_id}}:
1. Ads with frequency above 2.5 AND CTR down two weeks running -> refresh, pause, or widen.
2. For each underperforming ad, walk the funnel in order and name the FIRST broken stage:
   attention (CTR vs account baseline), click-to-land (landing page views vs link clicks),
   land-to-action (results vs landing page views), cost (CPM vs baseline),
   quality (good results vs total results).
3. Plot new ads launched vs spend over time — if spend grew while new creative did not,
   that is the whole problem.
Output: one line per ad, the broken stage, and the fix.
